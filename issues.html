Priorities

District 86 priorities

Hannah is focused on issues that affect daily life: access to care, protecting rural land and water, strengthening local schools, and making government answer to people instead of party politics.

Protect rural land, water, and working families

Defend the natural resources, farms, homes, and outdoor spaces that make Northeast Oklahoma livable for the next generation.

Improve practical access to care

Support rural clinics, nurses, emergency services, mental health access, and policies that reduce long drives and delays for basic care.

Put community needs over party pressure

Listen first, explain votes clearly, and focus on schools, roads, services, and household costs instead of political games.
